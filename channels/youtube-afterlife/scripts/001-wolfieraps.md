# YoutubeAfterLife #1: What Happened to WolfieRaps?

**Working title:** What Happened to WolfieRaps? The Scandal That Froze a 7 Million Sub Channel
**Length:** about 7 minutes (~1,050 words of narration)
**Tone:** calm documentary narrator, a little dramatic, never mocking. We report what was *alleged* and what was *said*. We don't decide who's guilty.

> **Rules for this video (keep it safe from strikes and lawsuits)**
> - Every claim about the 2018 situation is said as an **allegation**, and we always include that **Wolfie denied it**.
> - The girl who made the claims was reported as a teenager. We **never name her, show her face, or show her handle**. She's a blurred silhouette or a blurred phone screen only.
> - Cartoon versions of Wolfie and Sylvia are drawn in our own style (no traced photos, no real thumbnails, no clips from their channels).
> - Put the sources on screen when we use them (small text bottom-left).

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 - 0:25)
*[Black screen. A phone notification sound. A cartoon phone lights up: "DramaAlert: new video".]*

**NARRATOR:** In May 2018, one of the biggest prank couples on YouTube went quiet overnight.

*[Cartoon Wolfie and Sylvia on a couch, laughing at a camera. The picture cracks like glass.]*

**NARRATOR:** Seven million subscribers. Millions of views on every upload. And then one Instagram post changed everything.

*[Title card: tombstone with the halo. "WOLFIERAPS" carved in it. "WHAT HAPPENED?"]*

---

## PART 1: THE RISE (0:25 - 1:50)
*[Animated timeline. Small bedroom, a cheap camera on a tripod.]*

**NARRATOR:** Before the drama, there was Charlie Raynor. Online, everyone knew him as WolfieRaps, or just Wolfie.

**NARRATOR:** He started out like a lot of creators did back then: rapping, vlogging, trying anything that might stick. And then he found the thing that did: pranks. Specifically, pranks on his girlfriend, Sylvia Gani.

*[Cartoon montage: fake breakup prank, fake pregnancy prank, a "cheating" prank. Big red "PRANK!" stamps.]*

**NARRATOR:** It was the golden age of couple prank channels. Fake breakups. Fake cheating. Fake tears. The thumbnails were wild, the titles were in all caps, and the views were massive.

*[Subscriber counter spinning up: 1M... 3M... 5M... 7M.]*

**NARRATOR:** And Wolfie and Sylvia were one of the faces of it. Fans didn't just watch the pranks. They watched the relationship. They were invested in *them*.

**NARRATOR:** Which is exactly why what happened next hit so hard.

---

## PART 2: THE POST (1:50 - 3:20)
*[Dark screen. Blurred Instagram-style post. A faceless silhouette, no name visible.]*

**NARRATOR:** In May 2018, a girl on Instagram started posting about Wolfie.

**NARRATOR:** She claimed the two of them had been involved since September 2017, and that she'd met him at another YouTuber's party. She called herself his "side chick."

*[Blurred phone screenshots slide in. A text bubble with the name "Daddy" visible, the rest blurred.]*

**NARRATOR:** She shared what she said were flirty messages from him, with his contact saved as "Daddy," and a photo she said showed the two of them together.

*[Red text slams in: "SHE WAS REPORTED TO BE A TEENAGER."]*

**NARRATOR:** And here's the part that turned this from gossip into a full-blown scandal. Reports at the time said she was still in high school. Her exact age was disputed, and different posts online gave different numbers. But either way, the internet exploded.

*[Cartoon DramaAlert-style news desk (no real logo). Generic "DRAMA NEWS" banner.]*

**NARRATOR:** DramaAlert picked it up. The Daily Dot wrote about it. Reaction videos started piling up by the hour.

**NARRATOR:** And when Wolfie and Sylvia were asked for a comment...

*[Cartoon Wolfie and Sylvia behind a closed door. Silence. A clock ticking.]*

**NARRATOR:** They said nothing.

---

## PART 3: THE RESPONSE (3:20 - 4:30)
*[Cartoon Wolfie alone at night, phone light on his face.]*

**NARRATOR:** About a week later, Wolfie gave his side on Twitter. He denied cheating.

*[On screen, in quotes and small: "Wolfie denied the allegations." Source: Daily Dot / YouTube Wiki.]*

**NARRATOR:** But by then, the story was everywhere. And for a channel built on fake cheating pranks, a *real* cheating accusation was a nightmare. Every old prank thumbnail suddenly looked different. Comment sections filled up with the same question: was any of it ever real?

*[Cartoon Sylvia sitting alone, rain on a window.]*

**NARRATOR:** Then, on June 10th, 2018, Sylvia uploaded a video called "How to Survive Heartbreak."

**NARRATOR:** She didn't go into detail. She said she didn't want to involve herself in the drama. But fans read between the lines. The couple that built an empire on fake breakups... had actually broken up.

---

## PART 4: THE AFTERMATH (4:30 - 5:50)
*[Subscriber counter. The numbers stop climbing. It flickers, freezes.]*

**NARRATOR:** So did it end his career? Not exactly. But it changed it forever.

**NARRATOR:** The channel stopped growing the way it used to. Reports at the time noted he went quiet on uploads after the allegations. Years later, public stats show the channel sitting around seven million subscribers, a little lower than its peak. The rocket ship had stalled.

*[Cartoon Wolfie in a studio with headphones. A music note. Title "ON ME" on a screen.]*

**NARRATOR:** Wolfie leaned back into music. And in November 2018, Sylvia appeared in his music video, "On Me," which had fans asking whether they were back together.

**NARRATOR:** Reports say they officially got back together around October 2019. Not everyone was happy about it. Some fans forgave. Some never came back.

**NARRATOR:** Because the prank era was ending anyway. The audience grew up. The algorithm changed. And a lot of the channels from that era quietly faded out. Wolfie's just had a scandal attached to the fade.

---

## PART 5: WHERE ARE THEY NOW? (5:50 - 6:50)
*[Bright colours return. Cartoon Wolfie and Sylvia, older, calmer, holding hands.]*

**NARRATOR:** So where are they now?

**NARRATOR:** Still together. According to fan wikis, they got engaged in June 2024 and were later reported to have married.

*[Cartoon Sylvia with a baby bump. Wolfie holding a giant tub of ice cream, looking exhausted.]*

**NARRATOR:** And now they're expecting their first baby. These days, you're more likely to find Wolfie on Snapchat, making jokes about his pregnant wife's ice cream cravings, than filming a fake breakup prank.

**NARRATOR:** The guy who used to fake drama for views now just... posts his life.

---

## OUTRO (6:50 - 7:10)
*[Back to the tombstone. The halo flickers. "WOLFIERAPS" carved in it. Then the letters change to "STILL HERE."]*

**NARRATOR:** WolfieRaps didn't disappear. But the WolfieRaps that seven million people subscribed to? That version of the channel never really came back.

**NARRATOR:** Was it the scandal? The end of the prank era? Or both?

**NARRATOR:** Let us know in the comments. And tell us which YouTuber we should bring back from the afterlife next.

*[End screen: "SUBSCRIBE before we end up in the afterlife too."]*
