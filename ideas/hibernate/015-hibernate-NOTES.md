## Sources / fact check
- Black bear hibernation: heart rate under 20, sometimes under 10; urea recycling; little muscle or bone loss
- NASA: astronauts lose ~1-1.5% bone mass per month
- Dausmann et al., Nature 2004: fat-tailed dwarf lemur hibernates
- Takahashi et al., Nature 2020: Q neurons induce torpor in mice
- Targeted temperature management after cardiac arrest
- ESA and NASA/SpaceWorks studies of torpor for Mars crews
