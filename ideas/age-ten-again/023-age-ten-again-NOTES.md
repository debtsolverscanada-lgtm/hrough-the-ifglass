## Fact check
| Claim in script | Source |
|---|---|
| Foucault pendulum's swing plane rotates, demonstrating Earth's rotation | Léon Foucault, 1851; any intro physics text (e.g. Halliday, Resnick & Walker, *Fundamentals of Physics*) |
| Children's brains are more plastic; synapses are overproduced then pruned, used connections strengthened | Huttenlocher & Dabholkar (1997), *J. Comparative Neurology*; Harvard Center on the Developing Child, "Brain Architecture" |
| Starting a language before about age ten gives the best chance of native-like ability | Hartshorne, Tenenbaum & Pinker (2018), *Cognition* 177: native-like grammar most likely if learning starts by ~10 (learning ability itself stays high to ~17); Flege et al. on accent and age of arrival |
| Children aged 6 to 12 need 9 to 12 hours of sleep a night | Paruthi et al. (2016), American Academy of Sleep Medicine consensus, *J. Clinical Sleep Medicine* |
| At ~7% annual growth, money roughly doubles every ten years (Rule of 72) | Standard finance; U.S. SEC Investor.gov compound interest calculator / Rule of 72 explainer |
| Regular exercise strengthens the heart and lowers cardiovascular risk | WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity (2020); American Heart Association |
| Lorenz, 1961, rounded 0.506127 to 0.506 and got a completely different forecast | Lorenz (1963), "Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow", *J. Atmospheric Sciences*; Gleick, *Chaos* (1987) |
| Cardiac arrest: heart quivers (ventricular fibrillation) rather than pumping; gasping is not normal breathing | American Heart Association CPR & ECC Guidelines (2020) |
| Survival falls about 7 to 10% for each minute without CPR and defibrillation | American Heart Association; Larsen et al. (1993), *Annals of Emergency Medicine* |
| Adult compressions: 5 to 6 cm deep, 100 to 120 per minute | AHA 2020 Guidelines; European Resuscitation Council 2021 Guidelines |
| Younger children usually can't reach adult compression depth | Jones et al. (2007), *Archives of Disease in Childhood*: children under ~13 struggled to reach adequate depth |
| Bystander effect; fix it by assigning one specific person | Darley & Latané (1968), *J. Personality and Social Psychology*; first-aid training guidance (British Red Cross, St John Ambulance) |
| AEDs talk users through each step and only shock a shockable rhythm | Resuscitation Council UK, "A guide to Automated External Defibrillators" (2019); AHA |
