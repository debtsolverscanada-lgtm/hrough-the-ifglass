## Sources / fact check
- Dogs up to ~300M olfactory receptors vs ~5-6M in humans
- Hepper & Wells 2005: dogs tell trail direction from a few footsteps
- Wilson et al., PLOS ONE 2022: dogs detect human stress odour (~94%)
- Joy Milne / Trivedi et al. 2019: smelling Parkinson's
- Porter et al., Nature Neuroscience 2007: humans can follow a chocolate scent trail
