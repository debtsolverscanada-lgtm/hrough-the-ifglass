## Sources / fact check
- ~500M neurons, about 2/3 in the arms (Hochner; Godfrey-Smith)
- van Giesen et al., Cell 2020: chemotactile receptors in suckers
- Nesher et al., Current Biology 2014: skin chemical stops suckers grabbing own arms
- Ramirez & Oakley 2015: light-sensitive opsins in octopus skin
- Enteric nervous system: ~200-600M neurons ('second brain')
