## Fact check
- 2.4 GHz wavelength = 12.5 cm (5 GHz = 6 cm). Visible light is 400–700 nm, smaller than bacteria (1–2 µm).
- Eye resolution ≈ 1.22λ/D. With a 5 mm pupil at 550 nm that's about 1.3×10⁻⁴ rad. Matching it at 12.5 cm needs D ≈ 1.1 km.
- Drywall and wood barely block 2.4 GHz. Metal reflects it. Water absorbs it. The body is about 60% water.
- Microwave ovens run at 2.45 GHz and about 1,000 W. Routers run at about 0.1 W. Ovens leak slightly and can interfere with 2.4 GHz WiFi.
- Contactless cards are passive NFC (13.56 MHz), powered by the reader's field.
- FM radio (88–108 MHz) has a wavelength of about 3 m. Airport radar rotates every few seconds.
- GPS satellites orbit at about 20,200 km. Their signals arrive below the noise floor.
- The Sun emits radio bursts during flares.
- The cosmic microwave background is the remnant of the Big Bang, about 13.8 billion years old, and peaks in microwaves.
- WiFi and RF are non-ionizing. At consumer power levels the only known effect is slight heating (WHO/ICNIRP).
- RF sensing: MIT RF-Capture (through-wall), MIT Vital-Radio (breathing and heart rate, 2015), CMU "DensePose from WiFi" (2023).
