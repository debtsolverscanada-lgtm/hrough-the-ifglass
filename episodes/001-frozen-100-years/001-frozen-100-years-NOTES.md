## Fact check (sources to keep on file)
- Molly Gibson born 2020 from an embryo frozen in 1992 (about 27 years); her sister Emma (2017) came from the same embryo batch.
- University of Minnesota, 2023 (Nature Communications): rat kidneys vitrified for up to 100 days, nanowarmed with iron-oxide nanoparticles, transplanted, and they restored function.
- Chatsworth, California, 1970s: the Cryonics Society of California's patients thawed after the money ran out.
- UN World Population Prospects 2024: population peaks around 10.3 billion in the mid-2080s.
- James Bedford, frozen 1967, the first person cryopreserved; still stored at Alcor.
- About 500 people are cryopreserved worldwide (Alcor plus Cryonics Institute plus others).
- 1.07^100 ≈ 868 → $10,000 becomes about $8.68M.
