## Sources / fact check
- Flicker fusion: humans ~60 Hz, blowflies ~250 Hz
- Healy et al., Animal Behaviour 2013: smaller, faster animals perceive time faster
- Card & Dickinson 2008: fly escape manoeuvres
- Stetson, Fiesta & Eagleman, PLoS ONE 2007: falling does not speed up perception
