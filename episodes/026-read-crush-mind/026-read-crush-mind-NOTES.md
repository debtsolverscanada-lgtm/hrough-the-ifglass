## Fact check
- **Semantic decoder (UT Austin, 2023).** Tang, LeBel, Jain & Huth, "Semantic reconstruction of continuous language from non-invasive brain recordings", *Nature Neuroscience* 26: 858–866 (2023). fMRI; about 16 hours of training per participant listening to narrative podcasts; output captures the gist (UT Austin news release example: "I don't have my driver's license yet" decoded as "She has not even started to learn to drive yet"); decoders trained on one person did not work on another; cooperation required, and decoding was impaired when participants resisted with tasks such as counting by sevens, naming and imagining animals, or telling a different story. The authors stress mental privacy. The script's line "counting didn't switch the brain off, it just gave it something else to think about" is our plain-language reading of why a distraction task beats the decoder.
- **MEG decoding (Meta AI and others).** Défossez, Caucheteux, Rapin, Kabeli & King, "Decoding speech perception from non-invasive brain recordings", *Nature Machine Intelligence* 5: 1097–1107 (2023); Benchetrit, Banville & King, "Brain decoding: toward real-time reconstruction of visual perception" (ICLR 2024). MEG measures the brain's tiny magnetic fields with millisecond resolution ("thousands of measurements per second", Meta AI blog, Oct 2023).
- **MEG sensors are cooled with liquid helium** (about 4 K, minus 269 °C). Standard MEG engineering (e.g. Hämäläinen et al., *Reviews of Modern Physics* 65: 413, 1993). The cracked pipe and its effect on Glint are fiction.
- **Inner speech is condensed and fragmentary.** Vygotsky, *Thought and Language* (1934); Alderson-Day & Fernyhough, "Inner speech: development, cognitive functions, phenomenology, and neurobiology", *Psychological Bulletin* 141: 931–965 (2015). "Much faster than talking": condensed inner speech can run ahead of overt speech (Alderson-Day & Fernyhough 2015 review). The popular "4,000 words per minute" figure (Korba 1990, *Perceptual and Motor Skills* 71: 1043–1052) is a single weakly replicated estimate, so it is NOT used.
- **Inner speech in about a quarter of sampled moments.** Heavey & Hurlburt, "The phenomena of inner experience", *Consciousness and Cognition* 17: 798–810 (2008), Descriptive Experience Sampling: inner speaking in about 26% of samples, varying widely between people.
- **Some people have little or no inner voice.** Nedergaard & Lupyan, "Not everybody has an inner voice: behavioral consequences of anendophasia", *Psychological Science* 35 (2024); Hurlburt, Heavey & Kelsey (2013) on large individual differences.
- **Cocktail party effect and your own name.** Cherry, *J. Acoustical Society of America* 25: 975–979 (1953); Moray, *Quarterly J. Experimental Psychology* 11: 56–60 (1959); Wood & Cowan, *J. Exp. Psych.: Learning, Memory, and Cognition* 21: 255–260 (1995): about a third of participants noticed their own name in the ignored channel.
- **Sleep loss impairs like alcohol.** Dawson & Reid, *Nature* 388: 235 (1997): after 17 hours awake, performance similar to 0.05% blood alcohol; 24 hours similar to 0.10%. Williamson & Feyer, *Occupational and Environmental Medicine* 57: 649–655 (2000): 17–19 hours awake equal to or worse than 0.05% BAC.
- **Microsleeps, often unnoticed by the person.** Boyle et al., "Driver performance in the moments surrounding a microsleep", *Transportation Research Part F* 11: 126–136 (2008); NHTSA, Drowsy Driving. Three seconds at 70 mph (31 m/s) is about 94 m, roughly a football pitch (100–110 m).
- **Cold air and radio barely help.** Reyner & Horne, "Evaluation of 'in-car' countermeasures to sleepiness: cold air and radio", *Sleep* 21: 46–50 (1998): only minor, short-lived benefit. Advice from RoSPA/NHS/THINK!: stop and rest; don't drive drowsy.
- **Counting backwards by sevens** ("serial sevens") is a standard attention test and gets harder when tired (e.g. used in the Mini-Mental State Exam, Folstein et al. 1975). Osei's slips are story.
- **Saturn would float in water** (mean density about 0.69 g/cm³, less than water). NASA Saturn fact sheet.
- **Fiction flagged in-story:** the power ("In this world, Glint's brain has become the decoder"), the training-by-listening rule's application to people, all heard thoughts, Maya's power.
