# Thumbnail style (from Oct 30, 2026)

The target look is the 016 "I SEE EVERYTHING" thumbnail (`episodes/016-invisible/thumb4.html`).

## Look
- **Vibrant.** Saturated purple/magenta or teal backgrounds, a big warm light source (lamp, fire, screen glow), glow blobs, bokeh, and rim light on the characters. Never flat or dark grey.
- **One huge foreground character** cropped close (Glint or the hero), rim-lit in two colours, with a strong expression: sly grin, shock, terror.
- **A second plane** behind with the story moment, lit warm.
- **Characters** in soft 2D cartoon style (round faces, big almond eyes with lashes, wavy glossy hair, blush). Glint keeps the gold glasses and glowing red eyes.

## Hook
- **Headline:** 2–3 words, huge, extruded. One line white, one yellow (e.g. "I SEE / EVERYTHING").
- **Curiosity gap the video pays off.** Danger, a secret, getting caught, "what happens next".
- **Flirty or innuendo humour is fine when the story has a scene that supports it.** Examples: a couple caught in bed under the covers, a "DO NOT DISTURB" sign, a blush.
- **Keep people clothed.** Lingerie or nudity gets videos age-restricted and buried.
- **Don't promise something the episode doesn't have.** Viewers who feel tricked click away early, and the video gets pushed less.

## Don'ts
- No copying other creators' artwork.
- No text longer than 3 words.
- No more than 3 focal elements.
